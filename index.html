About Desert Surgical Staffing

Desert Surgical Staffing provides dependable surgical staffing solutions for healthcare facilities throughout Arizona. We help facilities maintain coverage during staffing shortages, vacations, call-outs, increased surgical volume, and other temporary staffing needs.


With specialized experience in ophthalmology and surgical services, Desert Surgical Staffing is committed to providing experienced, professional, and reliable surgical personnel.
